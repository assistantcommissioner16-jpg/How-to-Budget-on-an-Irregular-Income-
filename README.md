How to Budget on an Irregular Income When Your Pay Changes Every Month
How to Budget on an Irregular Income: Standard budgeting advice, which assumes that the same amount of money will show up in the account on the same day every month, is not available to most freelancers, gig workers, and commission earners. When one month’s income is $1,800 and the next is $5,200, a typical budget breaks suddenly. You either splurge during the prosperous months or panic during the difficult ones

Increasing the budget is not the answer. It entails setting up a system that separates your earnings and outlays so that the monthly swings don’t interfere with your day-to-day activities.
